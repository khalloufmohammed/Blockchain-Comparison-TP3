
# Public Blockchain

## 1. Definition

A public blockchain is a decentralized digital network that allows anyone to participate, read transactions and, under certain rules, validate transactions.

## 2. Main Characteristics

- Open access: anyone can join the network.
- Decentralization: no single organization controls the entire network.
- Transparency: transactions can be publicly verified.
- Security: cryptographic techniques protect transactions.
- Immutability: confirmed records are difficult to modify.

## 3. Transaction Validation

Transactions are validated by network participants called nodes. Depending on the blockchain, consensus mechanisms such as Proof of Work or Proof of Stake are used.

## 4. Advantages

- Transparency
- Decentralization
- Security
- No central authority

## 5. Disadvantages

- Possible scalability limitations
- Transaction fees
- Energy consumption in Proof of Work systems
- Transaction processing can take time

## 6. Examples

- Bitcoin
- Ethereum

## 7. Conclusion

Public blockchains provide an open and transparent way to exchange and verify digital information without depending on a central authority.
