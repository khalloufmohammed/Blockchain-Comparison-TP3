
# Permissioned Blockchain

## 1. Definition

A permissioned blockchain is a distributed digital network where access is restricted to authorized participants.

## 2. Main Characteristics

- Restricted access: only approved participants can join.
- Identity management: participants have known identities.
- Access control: permissions define what users can do.
- Shared records: authorized participants share the same ledger.
- Governance: an organization or group manages network rules.

## 3. Transaction Validation

Transactions are validated by selected and authorized participants according to predefined rules.

## 4. Advantages

- Better control over access
- Greater privacy
- Faster transaction processing in many implementations
- Clear responsibility of participants

## 5. Disadvantages

- Less decentralization
- Dependence on network governance
- More complex administration
- Trust is required between participants

## 6. Examples

- Hyperledger Fabric
- R3 Corda

## 7. Conclusion

Permissioned blockchains are useful for organizations that need shared records, controlled access and collaboration between known participants.
